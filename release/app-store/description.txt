Keep an eye on your 3D prints from a simple native app and widget.

See progress, remaining time, the current job, layers, and nozzle, bed, and chamber temperatures. Add multiple printers and choose the printer shown by each widget.

Print Progress connects directly to supported local printer interfaces:
• Bambu Lab LAN status
• Klipper / Moonraker
• OctoPrint
• PrusaLink
• Duet / RepRapFirmware
• Creality K1-series native status
• SDCP v3

Compatibility depends on firmware and the interface your printer exposes. Not every model supplies every reading. Bambu H2D and Creality K1 Max have been checked on hardware; the other interfaces have synthetic automated coverage and may need firmware-specific adjustments.

On Mac, choose an optional menu bar progress circle, circle with percentage, percentage only, or remaining time. Click it to see full printer details.

New to the app? Follow the setup walkthrough or explore the built-in demo without a printer.

No account, subscription, advertising, or developer-operated cloud service. Connection settings and cached readings stay in your device's Keychain. Monitoring is read-only.

Your device must be able to reach the printer over a local network or a suitable VPN. Native widget refreshes are scheduled by the operating system and may be delayed. Open the app for a fresh reading. The app does not control printers or provide remote cloud access.

An independent open-source project, not affiliated with printer manufacturers.
